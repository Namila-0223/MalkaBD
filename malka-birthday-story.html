Chapter One 🌊

Where the Ocean Knows Your Name

Every morning you wake up, the sea is already waiting for you —
calling your name in the rhythm of its waves.

You walk barefoot on warm sand, letting the foam kiss your feet,
watching the horizon glow gold and endless.

This is where you are most yourself.

Chapter Two 🌸

A Garden That Blooms for You

Flowers lean toward you the way sunflowers chase the sun.
There's something about you — the way you pause to really see a bloom,
to notice the curve of a petal, the blush of a rose.

You don't just love flowers — you understand them.

And today, every petal in the world is celebrating you.

Chapter Three
☕

The Perfect Slow Morning

You wrap both hands around a warm cup,
breathe in the deep, rich aroma that says:
this moment is yours.

No rush. Just you, your thoughts, and the quiet magic
that lives inside a perfectly brewed cup of coffee.

Some people collect moments — you collect mornings.

Chapter Four 🎨

You See the World in Color

Where others see a blank canvas, you see possibility.
Your hands know how to take something ordinary
and make it alive — full of texture, story, and soul.

Art isn't something you do.
It's something you are.

Every brushstroke, every creation — a little piece of Malka
left beautifully in the world.

Chapter Five 🎂

Today Is All Yours, Malka

A girl who loves the ocean, flowers, coffee, and art —
a girl who finds beauty in everything she touches.

Happy Birthday.
May this year bring you waves of joy,
gardens of laughter, mornings of warmth,
and a canvas full of your most beautiful dreams.

"Keep chasing sunsets, keep collecting flowers,
keep savoring those slow mornings,
keep creating beautiful things —
because the world is brighter with you in it."

— With all the love 💛
